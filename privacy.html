LucProductivityApp privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

LucProductivityApp is used only by its owner, Lucian Moldovanu, with his own Google account.

Google data accessed

  • Google Drive (read-only): to list and copy his own files with rclone.
  • Google Calendar events: to create, update and delete events in calendars he owns.

How it is used and stored

Data is processed only on the owner's own devices. OAuth tokens are stored only on those devices. No data is collected by, stored with, sold to, or shared with any third party, and none is used for advertising or to train AI models.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
